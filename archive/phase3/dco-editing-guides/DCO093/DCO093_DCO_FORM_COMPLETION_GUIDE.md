## About This Guide

This guide tells you exactly what to enter into the FM1-QM.SLQ001 Rev B Document Change Order Form to execute the DCO093 package. It is the companion to DCO093_PHASE2_EDITING_GUIDE.md (the document-by-document content edits). This guide covers the change order paperwork only.

Every field below is presented in the order it appears on the Rev B form. Text shown in fixed blocks is copy/paste ready. No asterisk characters are used anywhere in this guide, so paste blocks can be moved directly into the controlled Word form without cleanup. Where a value must be chosen by the originator (for example a calendar date or a signature), the guide marks it as a fill-in and gives a recommendation.

The FM1-QM.SLQ001 Rev B layout (for orientation) is:
- Header strip: DCO number, Change Priority, DCO Type, Document Category, Originator. The legacy Req. Effective Date R-plus field was removed in Rev B.
- Document listing table: Document Title, Document / Part Number, Current Revision, Proposed effective date (calendar, GMP use), Description of / Reason for Change.
- Originator attestation block (five checkboxes) below the document table.
- Additional Risk Assessment required (Yes / No, plus a table if Yes).
- Verification or Validation required (Yes / No, plus a table if Yes).
- Training required (Yes / No, plus a table if Yes).
- Material disposition required (Yes / No, plus a table if Yes).
- Potential Regulatory Impact (Yes / No, plus a Regulatory Assessment box if Yes).
- Approvals table (Printed Name, Department, Signature/Date).

## Part 1 — Header Strip

Enter the following values in the header strip of the form.

- DCO number: DCO093
- Change Priority: Medium
- DCO Type: Permanent DCO
- Document Category: check Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and Work Instruction (WI). DCO093 also revises two forms and one controlled log; if the form does not provide a Form or Log category checkbox, leave SOP and WI checked and rely on the document listing table to identify the forms and the log by number.
- Originator: Ethan Rao

Notes on the choices above:
- Change Priority Medium is recommended because DCO093 closes internal audit findings (IA-2025) and implements planned Quality Plan and EDMS-transition improvements on a managed schedule rather than in response to an active field or safety emergency. If SILQ wishes to tie DCO093 to the FDA 483 response timeline referenced in CAPA 002 and CAPA 003, High is also defensible; select one and keep it consistent with the CAPA records.
- DCO Type Permanent DCO is correct because these are permanent procedure revisions. Do not select Permanent DCO - Administrative; several changes are substantive (CAPA workflow restructure, complaint trend escalation criteria, confidential information security model) and exceed administrative scope.

## Part 4 — Originator Attestation Block

The Rev B form carries the originator attestation block below the document table. Mark the five checkboxes and add the short note where indicated. For DCO093 the recommended responses are below.

Attestation statement (already printed on the Rev B form; no entry needed): the originator confirms that each proposed calendar effective date allows controlled implementation, including consideration of patient and product risk, completion of required training before GMP use where training applies, regulatory timing when applicable, supplier or contract manufacturer readiness, field or inventory implementation when applicable, and alignment with material disposition where applicable.

Check all five boxes and use the following note text:

- Risk level and any needed mitigation timing reviewed for the change: check. Note: DCO093 changes are procedural QMS revisions. They do not alter device design, specifications, materials, labeling, or manufacturing process and therefore do not change the product risk profile.
- Training required before GMP use is scheduled to complete before the earliest proposed effective date for affected roles, or justification recorded: check. Note: read-and-acknowledge or interactive training is assigned per Part 7. The proposed effective date is set on or after planned training completion for the affected roles.
- Regulatory or certification timing constraints considered: check. Note: changes align procedures with the current FDA QMSR and applicable reporting regulations. No premarket submission or external regulatory notification is required to make these revisions effective.
- Supplier, CMO, or internal implementation readiness considered: check. Note: no supplier or contract manufacturer action is required. Implementation is internal to the SILQ QMS.
- Field or distribution implementation considered when relevant: check. Note: no field or distribution action is required; no product in the field is affected by these procedure revisions.

## Part 5 — Proposed Effective Dates (calendar, GMP use)

Enter one calendar date in the Proposed effective date column for each document row. The following grouping constraints apply because of cross-document dependencies. Confirm the dates with approvers before signature.

Mandatory same-date groups (these items reference one another and must share a single effective date):
- Group A: QM.SLQ016, FM1-QM.SLQ016, and the SILQ CAPA Log. The SOP, its form, and its log implement one CAPA workflow and must go effective together.
- Group B: QM.SLQ018 and FM1-QM.SLQ018. The SOP requires the annual input coverage confirmation that the form provides.

Recommended approach: set a single common proposed effective date for the entire DCO093 package on or after the date training is scheduled to complete for the affected roles. A single package date is the simplest controlled option and satisfies both grouping constraints above. If a staggered rollout is preferred, keep Group A on one shared date and Group B on one shared date, and you may date the remaining standalone SOPs (QM.SLQ012, QM.SLQ013, QM.SLQ021, QM.SLQ022, QM.SLQ023, QM.SLQ028, QM.SLQ030) individually.

Fill-in required: [enter the calendar effective date or dates]. Do not leave any row blank; the Rev B process requires a calendar date per row and the document administrator will return the DCO if a row date is missing.

## Part 6 — Additional Risk Assessment Required

Answer: No.

If the form requires the rationale to be recorded when No is selected, or if SILQ practice is to document the basis, use:

Rationale: The DCO093 changes are revisions to QMS procedures, forms, and a controlled log. They do not modify device design, specifications, materials, components, labeling, or manufacturing or inspection processes, and therefore do not introduce new product hazards or alter the probability or severity of existing hazards. No additional product or process risk assessment is required to implement these document changes. Note that QM.SLQ012 and QM.SLQ013 are themselves risk management procedures; the edits clarify and strengthen those procedures and do not require a product risk assessment to take effect.

## Part 7 — Training Required

Answer: Yes.

Complete the training table with the rows below. Notification Type is either Read and Acknowledge only or Interactive Training. Effectiveness Check is Yes or No. The recommendations differentiate substantive process changes (interactive training with an effectiveness check) from clarifications and administrative or reference updates (read and acknowledge).

| Applicable Doc No. | Notification Type | Effectiveness Check |
|---|---|---|
| QM.SLQ016 and FM1-QM.SLQ016 | Interactive Training | Yes |
| QM.SLQ021 | Interactive Training | Yes |
| QM.SLQ028 | Interactive Training | Yes |
| QM.SLQ013 | Read and Acknowledge only | Yes |
| QM.SLQ012 | Read and Acknowledge only | No |
| QM.SLQ018 and FM1-QM.SLQ018 | Read and Acknowledge only | No |
| QM.SLQ022 | Read and Acknowledge only | Yes |
| QM.SLQ023 | Read and Acknowledge only | No |
| QM.SLQ030 | Read and Acknowledge only | No |

Notes:
- QM.SLQ016, QM.SLQ021, and QM.SLQ028 carry the most substantive process changes (CAPA workflow restructure, complaint trend escalation, and the confidential information security model). Interactive training with an effectiveness check is recommended for the personnel who execute these procedures.
- QM.SLQ013 and QM.SLQ022 carry mandatory or substantive content changes (design-change risk evaluation, MDR reportability framework) for which an effectiveness check is recommended even with read-and-acknowledge delivery.
- The SILQ CAPA Log is trained as part of the QM.SLQ016 and FM1-QM.SLQ016 interactive training and does not require a separate training row.
- Training methodology and any comprehension assessment follow QM.SLQ003. Schedule training to complete before the proposed effective dates in Part 5.

Answer: Yes.

## Part 11 — After Approval: Publication in Silq eQMS

After all approvals are complete, the document administrator publishes each line item. These steps follow QM.SLQ001 Rev B and QM.SLQ014 Rev C.

1. For each row on the executed DCO, read the Document / Part Number, the new revision, and the final approved Proposed effective date.
2. In Silq eQMS Document Control, open that document number and create or select the draft revision matching the DCO.
3. On Release revision, set the Effective date to the same calendar date recorded for that row on the executed DCO. Do not apply any default offset from the approval date.
4. Confirm the Group A items (QM.SLQ016, FM1-QM.SLQ016, SILQ CAPA Log) carry the same effective date, and the Group B items (QM.SLQ018, FM1-QM.SLQ018) carry the same effective date.
5. Migrate the three existing CAPA rows in the SILQ CAPA Log to the revised column structure using the old-to-new column mapping in DCO093_PHASE2_EDITING_GUIDE.md, section 6 of Document 3.
6. Notify the Training Coordinator to initiate the training assignments recorded in Part 7 so that training completes before the effective dates.
7. File the executed DCO093 PDF in the Completed DCO location named in QM.SLQ001 so that the DCO row dates can be compared to the Silq eQMS revision effective dates.
