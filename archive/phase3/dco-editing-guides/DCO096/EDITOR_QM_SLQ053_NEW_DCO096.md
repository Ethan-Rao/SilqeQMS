Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines SILQ's process for identifying employee training requirements, providing training to personnel performing activities affecting product quality or the quality management system, documenting training completion, and evaluating training effectiveness. This procedure is structured to comply with ISO 13485:2016 Section 6.2 and 21 CFR Part 820 Section 820.25. Training records are maintained within the Silq eQMS electronic quality management system.

### SECTION 4 — DEFINITIONS

---PASTE START---
Definitions

Abbreviations and Acronyms

CAPA:     Corrective and Preventive Action
DCO:      Document Change Order
FDA:      United States Food and Drug Administration
GDP:      Good Documentation Practices
QMS:      Quality Management System
SOP:      Standard Operating Procedure

General Definitions

Annual Effectiveness Review:  A written assessment administered by the Training Coordinator once per calendar year to verify employee comprehension across their current training scope. The assessment contains approximately 10 role-relevant questions drawn from the employee's current training requirements; a score of 80% or above constitutes a passing result. Results are documented and retained in the Silq eQMS.

Base Training Requirements:  The set of controlled documents for which all SILQ employees are required to maintain current training, regardless of role. Base Requirements are identified in the Training Matrix (Appendix 1) and include the Quality Policy, Quality Manual, Good Documentation Practices SOP, and this SOP.

Employee Training Program:  A role-specific list of controlled documents and procedures for which an employee is required to complete and maintain training. The current Training Matrix (Appendix 1) defines training requirements by role and is maintained live in the Silq eQMS.

Employee Training Record:  The timestamped acknowledgment record generated in the Silq eQMS when an employee completes a training assignment, or the DCO approval record when a DCO approver is auto-qualified per Section 8. For interactive training, a brief written record submitted to the Training Coordinator and retained in the eQMS constitutes the training record.

Read and Acknowledge:  A training method in which the employee independently reads the assigned document or document revision and then clicks Acknowledge within the Silq eQMS. The system records the employee name, document revision, and timestamp. This record constitutes a complete training record for Read and Acknowledge training.

Interactive Training:  A training method in which the employee interfaces directly with a trainer (for example, one-on-one instruction, group training, or hands-on demonstration). A record of interactive training completion is required per Section 7.3.

Silq eQMS:  The SILQ electronic quality management system — a custom web-based platform used for document control, training assignments, acknowledgment records, and audit trails.

Trainer:  An individual who possesses appropriate knowledge of the subject matter and has been approved by the Training Coordinator to conduct interactive training. Document originators and DCO approvers are considered trained on the documents they have originated or approved and may act as trainers for those documents.

Training Coordinator:  Ethan Rao, Director of R&D, QA, and RA, in his capacity as the designated Quality Assurance lead. The Training Coordinator is responsible for maintaining the Training Matrix, creating training assignments in the Silq eQMS, administering the Annual Effectiveness Review, and ensuring training records are complete and accessible.
---PASTE END---

### SECTION 5 — RESPONSIBILITIES

---PASTE START---
Responsibilities

5.1 Training Coordinator (Ethan Rao):  The Training Coordinator is responsible for maintaining the Training Matrix (Appendix 1); creating and managing training assignments in the Silq eQMS; administering the Annual Effectiveness Review; ensuring training records are accurate, complete, and accessible; and updating training requirements when job responsibilities or controlled documents change.

5.2 Department Management:  Each employee's functional manager is responsible for ensuring their direct reports complete assigned training within specified timeframes and for notifying the Training Coordinator when an employee's responsibilities change or a new employee joins their team.

5.3 All Employees:  All employees are responsible for completing training assignments in the Silq eQMS by the specified due dates; achieving a passing score on the Annual Effectiveness Review; asking for clarification when training material is not understood; and refraining from independently performing tasks governed by a procedure for which they have not completed training, unless supervised by a currently trained individual.

5.4 Silq eQMS System Administrator (Initial Alignment Period):  During the initial training alignment period following the effective date of this SOP, the Silq eQMS system administrator has full authority to create, modify, and backdate training assignments on behalf of any user to support the organizational transition to the Silq eQMS. This authority is limited to the initial alignment period, which concludes when the Training Coordinator (Ethan Rao) confirms in writing that initial training alignment is complete. An email or an eQMS note is sufficient documentation of this confirmation.

5.5 Contractors and Consultants:  Contractors and consultants are responsible for completing their project-specific onboarding training assignment within 10 business days of engagement start, in accordance with Section 13 of this procedure.

### SECTION 6 — PROCEDURE: TRAINING MATRIX

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Training Matrix

6.1 The SILQ Training Matrix defines, by role, the controlled documents and procedures for which each employee is required to maintain current training. The current Training Matrix is maintained in Appendix 1 of this SOP and is kept current in the Silq eQMS training module.

6.2 The Training Coordinator reviews and updates the Training Matrix at minimum annually or whenever: a new controlled document is released; an employee's job responsibilities change; a new employee joins the organization; or an employee's role is modified.

6.3 The Training Matrix is the authoritative source for determining training requirements. Individual employee training assignments are derived from it and managed within the Silq eQMS.
---PASTE END---

---

### SECTION 7 — PROCEDURE: TRAINING ASSIGNMENT AND ACKNOWLEDGMENT

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Training Assignment and Acknowledgment

7.1 The Training Coordinator creates training assignments in the Silq eQMS for each employee based on the Training Matrix (Appendix 1) and any additional requirements specified on DCO forms. Training assignments include a link to the relevant document revision and a due date.

7.2 The default training type for all QMS document training is Read and Acknowledge. The employee opens the linked document revision in the Silq eQMS, reads it, and clicks Acknowledge. The system records the employee name, document revision, and timestamp. This acknowledgment record constitutes the complete training record; no separate paper or digital form is required for Read and Acknowledge training.

7.3 Interactive training is required only when explicitly designated on the DCO form by the DCO originator, or when directed by the Training Coordinator for hands-on manufacturing or laboratory procedures. When interactive training is required: the trainer reviews the material with the trainee; upon completion, the trainer records completion in the Silq eQMS (if system access permits) or submits a brief written note containing the employee name, document title, document revision, date, and trainer name to the Training Coordinator, who uploads it as the training record in the eQMS.

7.4 Employees may not independently perform tasks governed by a procedure for which they have not completed training, unless supervised by a currently trained individual. The supervising trainer countersigns any associated quality records generated during the supervised activity.

7.5 Training assignments carry a default due date of 30 days from the assignment date, unless a different deadline is specified on the DCO form or by the Training Coordinator.

7.6 The Training Coordinator may assign training to documents not listed in an employee's current training profile when operational needs require it. Such training follows the standard Read and Acknowledge process and is logged in the Silq eQMS.
---PASTE END---

### SECTION 8 — PROCEDURE: DCO SIGNER AUTO-QUALIFICATION

---PASTE START---
Procedure: DCO Signer Auto-Qualification

8.1 Any employee who reviews and approves a Document Change Order (DCO) in the Silq eQMS is automatically deemed trained on every controlled document revision covered by that DCO. This qualification takes effect on the DCO approval date.

8.2 The DCO approval record in the Silq eQMS serves as the training record for auto-qualified documents. The Training Coordinator is not required to create a separate training assignment for DCO approvers on documents covered by that DCO.

8.3 A document originator is deemed trained on the document they authored, effective on the document's release date. The Training Coordinator is not required to create a separate training assignment for document originators on the documents they have written.
---PASTE END---

---

### SECTION 9 — PROCEDURE: RETRAINING ON DOCUMENT CHANGES

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Retraining on Document Changes

9.1 When a controlled document is revised, the DCO originator specifies the training requirements (affected roles and training type) on the DCO form. The Training Coordinator creates the corresponding training assignments in the Silq eQMS for the affected employee population per the Training Matrix.

9.2 Employees listed as DCO approvers on the associated DCO are auto-qualified per Section 8 and do not require a separate retraining assignment for documents covered by that DCO.

9.3 Affected employees must complete retraining within the timeframe specified on the DCO form. If no timeframe is specified, the default is 30 days from the document's effective date.

9.4 Ad hoc retraining may be initiated at any time by the Training Coordinator or department management in response to a quality issue, CAPA finding, or identified knowledge gap.
---PASTE END---

### SECTION 10 — PROCEDURE: ANNUAL QUALITY POLICY RE-ACKNOWLEDGMENT

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Annual Quality Policy Re-Acknowledgment

10.1 In January of each calendar year, the Training Coordinator creates a Read and Acknowledge training assignment for the current revision of the SILQ Quality Policy (QM.SLQ035) and assigns it to all active employees.

10.2 Employees must complete the re-acknowledgment by February 28 of the same year.

10.3 The Silq eQMS acknowledgment record serves as the annual Quality Policy training record. No separate form is required.
---PASTE END---

### SECTION 11 — PROCEDURE: ANNUAL EFFECTIVENESS REVIEW

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Annual Effectiveness Review

11.1 Once per calendar year, the Training Coordinator administers a written Annual Effectiveness Review to each active employee.

11.2 The review consists of approximately 10 questions drawn from the employee's current training scope as defined in the Training Matrix (Appendix 1) and tailored to the employee's role and responsibilities. The Training Coordinator prepares the questions; they may be delivered as a printed or electronic document.

11.3 A score of 80% or above (8 of 10 questions correct, or proportional equivalent for reviews with a different number of questions) constitutes a passing result.

11.4 The Training Coordinator documents each review: employee name, date of review, score, and pass or fail result. The completed record is uploaded to the employee's training folder in the Silq eQMS.

11.5 If an employee scores below 80%, the Training Coordinator conducts a targeted review of the failed topic areas with the employee and administers a single re-assessment. The re-assessment is documented as a separate record. Persistent failure is escalated to department management.

11.6 The first Annual Effectiveness Review for the current team is to be completed no later than December 31, 2026.
---PASTE END---

### SECTION 12 — PROCEDURE: NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

---PASTE START---
Procedure: New Employee Onboarding

12.1 Upon hiring, the Training Coordinator creates training assignments for the new employee in the Silq eQMS, covering: (a) Base Requirements — Quality Policy (QM.SLQ035), Employee Training Program SOP (QM.SLQ053), Good Documentation Practices SOP (QM.SLQ002), and Quality Manual (QM.SLQ027); and (b) role-specific documents per the Training Matrix (Appendix 1).

12.2 Base Requirements training must be completed within 30 days of the employee's start date.

12.3 Role-specific training must be completed within 60 days of the employee's start date.

12.4 New employees who join mid-year are included in the next scheduled Annual Effectiveness Review cycle.

12.5 Documentation of the employee's qualifications — such as resume, credentials, or relevant licenses — is maintained in the employee's personnel file.
---PASTE END---

### SECTION 13 — PROCEDURE: CONTRACTOR AND CONSULTANT TRAINING

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Contractor and Consultant Training

13.1 Prior to or within 10 business days of commencing work for SILQ, each contractor or consultant performing activities that may affect product quality or the quality management system must acknowledge the SILQ Quality Policy (QM.SLQ035) and any procedures directly relevant to their scope of work.

13.2 The Training Coordinator creates the appropriate Read and Acknowledge assignments in the Silq eQMS. If the contractor or consultant does not have Silq eQMS access, the Training Coordinator provides document copies with a written acknowledgment form. The signed acknowledgment form is uploaded to the eQMS as the training record.

13.3 No Annual Effectiveness Review is required for contractors or consultants.
---PASTE END---

---

### SECTION 14 — PROCEDURE: TRAINING RECORDS AND RETRIEVAL

---PASTE START---
Procedure: Training Records and Retrieval

14.1 All training records are maintained within the Silq eQMS training module. Records include: document title, document revision, employee name, training type, completion date, and (for Annual Effectiveness Reviews) score and pass or fail result.

14.2 Training records are retained in accordance with SILQ's document retention schedule and applicable regulatory requirements.

14.3 Training records are accessible to authorized users via the Silq eQMS. The Training Coordinator may export or print records for audit purposes at any time.

14.4 Monthly training matrix printouts and monthly discrepancy reports are not required. The Training Coordinator monitors training compliance on an ongoing basis using Silq eQMS dashboards and reports, and conducts a formal review at minimum annually or when an employee's role changes.
---PASTE END---
