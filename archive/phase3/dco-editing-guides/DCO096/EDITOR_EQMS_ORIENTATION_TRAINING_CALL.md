What Is Silq eQMS?

Silq eQMS is SILQ's electronic quality management system — a web-based platform that
replaces FileHold as SILQ's document management system.

What it does:
  - Stores and manages all controlled QMS documents (SOPs, work instructions, forms)
  - Tracks your training assignments and acknowledgment records
  - Gives your team visibility into manufacturing lots, equipment status, purchasing,

### SLIDE 4 — My Training Module

Layout: Bullet slide. Walk through this live on screen while presenting.

---PASTE START---
Completing Your Training — My Training Module

Your training assignments are in My Training (Dashboard → QMS System → My Training).

Each assignment shows:
  - The document you need to read
  - A due date
  - An Open button to view the document and an Acknowledge button to confirm completion

How to complete a training assignment:
  1. Click Open (or View Document) to open and read the document
  2. When finished, click Acknowledge
  3. The system records your name, document revision, and timestamp — that is your
     training record. No paper form is required.

You are responsible for completing all assigned training by the due dates shown.
If a document is unclear, ask Ethan before the due date — do not skip it.
---PASTE END---
