# Quality Objectives (QM.SLQ037) — Management Alignment Sheet

Prepared by: Ethan Rao, Quality Assurance
Date: June 24, 2026
For: Management team review and alignment

## Why we are doing this

We are revising QM.SLQ037 Quality Objectives to do three things: tighten our two existing objectives, add three new objectives, and add clear rules for how objectives are trended, reviewed, and escalated. This closes internal audit finding mNC 14 from IA-2025 and completes the Quality Objectives action items in the 2026 Quality Plan.

Before we execute the document change, we need the management team to agree on the five objectives below. Once aligned, we will finalize QM.SLQ037 as part of DCO094.

## The five objectives we are asking you to approve

1. Incoming material quality (Owner: QA Management)
   - Today: lot acceptance rate greater than 80 percent.
   - Proposed: lot acceptance rate of at least 90 percent, measured as a rolling twelve-month rate.
   - If we fall short: below 85 percent for two consecutive quarters opens a CAPA evaluation.

2. Finished product quality (Owner: Chief Technology Officer)
   - Today: complaint rate less than 5 percent of distributed catheters.
   - Proposed: complaint rate less than 1 percent of distributed catheters, measured as a rolling twelve-month rate.
   - If we fall short: a complaint rate that reaches or exceeds 1 percent in any rolling twelve-month period, or any single complaint category up 50 percent versus the prior period, opens a CAPA.

3. Active Post-Market Surveillance (new) (Owner: Chuck Greiner, with QA support)
   - Proposed: complete at least 12 active post-market surveillance activities per calendar year.
   - Activities include, but are not limited to: an in-person in-servicing visit to an existing customer; soliciting feedback after a customer's initial order; attending vendor events in person or virtually; and initiating a product evaluation project with a new customer.
   - If we fall short: fewer than 6 activities completed by the end of Q2 is flagged at the quarterly review.

4. Dynamic Employee Training Program (new) (Owner: Ethan Rao, QA)
   - Proposed: complete at least 10 employee training program activities per calendar year.
   - Activities include, but are not limited to: establishing a new comprehension assessment; updating an existing comprehension assessment to reflect a recent procedure change; and a successful quarterly review confirming all training requirements were completed on time.
   - If we fall short: fewer than 5 activities completed by the end of Q2 is flagged at the quarterly review.

5. Quarterly Execution of Quality Plan Objectives (new) (Owner: Director, R&D / QA / RA)
   - Proposed: at least 80 percent of Quality Plan action items are on or ahead of schedule at each quarterly review, evaluated against a minimum of 5 action items.
   - If we fall short: below 80 percent at any quarterly review opens a corrective action.

## Plain-language notes

- Incoming material: 80 percent is a low bar. 90 percent is a meaningful tightening that should still be achievable. One request: please confirm the 90 percent figure against our last twelve months of receiving inspection data. If we routinely run higher, we should set the bar higher.
- Complaint rate: our actual complaint rate is and has been under 1 percent, so the old "less than 5 percent" target told us nothing useful. A "less than 1 percent" target reflects our real performance. Because a low complaint count can simply mean less customer contact rather than better quality, we deliberately pair this objective with the new Active Post-Market Surveillance objective so that a good complaint number is backed by real surveillance activity.
- The two activity-based objectives (Active Surveillance and Training) are counted as a number of completed activities per year so they are concrete and easy to track at each quarterly review, rather than open-ended.

## What we need from you

- Agreement on the five goals above.
- Confirmation (or adjustment) of the 90 percent incoming acceptance figure against historical data.

On your alignment, we will finalize QM.SLQ037 and route it for approval within DCO094.
