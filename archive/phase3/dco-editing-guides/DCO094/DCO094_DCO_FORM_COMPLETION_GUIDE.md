## About This Guide

This guide tells you exactly what to enter into the FM1-QM.SLQ001 Rev B Document Change Order Form to execute the DCO094 package. It is the companion to DCO094_PHASE2_EDITING_GUIDE.md (the document-by-document content edits). This guide covers the change order paperwork only.

Every field below is presented in the order it appears on the Rev B form. Text shown in fixed blocks is copy/paste ready. No asterisk characters are used anywhere in this guide, so paste blocks can be moved directly into the controlled Word form without cleanup. Where a value must be chosen by the originator (for example a calendar date or a signature), the guide marks it as a fill-in and gives a recommendation.

## Part 1 — Header Strip

Enter the following values in the header strip of the form.

- DCO number: DCO094
- Change Priority: Medium
- DCO Type: Permanent DCO
- Document Category: Check Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and Work Instruction (WI). DCO094 also revises two forms (FM4-QM.SLQ050 and FM1-QM.SLQ040); if the form does not provide a Form category checkbox, leave SOP and WI checked and rely on the document listing table to identify the forms by number.
- Originator: Ethan Rao

Notes on the choices above:

- Change Priority Medium is recommended. DCO094 closes internal audit findings (IA-2025) and implements planned Quality Plan and EDMS-transition improvements on a managed schedule. The changes are not in response to an active field or safety emergency. If Ethan wishes to tie DCO094 to the FDA 483 response timeline referenced in CAPA 002 or CAPA 003, High is also defensible; select one and keep it consistent with the CAPA records.
- DCO Type Permanent DCO is correct because these are permanent procedure revisions. Do not select Permanent DCO - Administrative; several changes are substantive (mNC 11 DMR ownership, mNC 12 out-of-tolerance objectivity, mNC 15 nonconforming material documentation, mNC 9 and 10 and 14, Medical Device File framework) and exceed administrative scope.

## Part 5 — Proposed Effective Dates (calendar, GMP use)

Enter one calendar date in the Proposed effective date column for each document row. The following same-date grouping constraints apply because of cross-document dependencies.

Mandatory same-date groups:

- Group A: QM.SLQ027 and QM.SLQ048. These two documents implement the Medical Device File cross-reference together (QM.SLQ027 defines the construct; QM.SLQ048 provides the index). They must share a single effective date.
- Group B: QM.SLQ050 and FM4-QM.SLQ050. The SOP revision (mNC 12 out-of-tolerance requirements) references the revised form. They must share a single effective date.
- Group C: QM.SLQ040 and FM1-QM.SLQ040. The SOP revision (mNC 15 use-as-is risk assessment requirement) references the revised form. They must share a single effective date.

Recommended approach: Set a single common proposed effective date for the entire DCO094 package on or after the date interactive training is scheduled to complete for the substantive changes. A single package date is the simplest controlled option and satisfies all three grouping constraints above. If a staggered rollout is preferred, maintain the three groups above on shared dates, and you may date the remaining EDMS-only SOPs (QM.SLQ029, QM.SLQ038, QM.SLQ039, QM.SLQ043, QM.SLQ045, QM.SLQ046, QM.SLQ049, QM.SLQ051) individually or as a second batch.

Fill-in required: [enter the calendar effective date or dates]. Do not leave any row blank; the Rev B process requires a calendar date per row.

## Part 6 — Additional Risk Assessment Required

Answer: No.

Rationale: The DCO094 changes are revisions to QMS procedures and two associated forms. They do not modify device design, specifications, materials, components, labeling, or manufacturing or inspection processes, and therefore do not introduce new product hazards or alter the probability or severity of existing hazards. No additional product or process risk assessment is required to implement these document changes. The changes to QM.SLQ050 and FM4-QM.SLQ050 strengthen calibration controls; the mNC 11 changes to QM.SLQ048 clarify legal manufacturer DMR ownership. Neither constitutes a product design or manufacturing change.

- QM.SLQ027 and QM.SLQ048: The Medical Device File framework is a new construct for SILQ personnel and auditors. - QM.SLQ033: New escalation criteria and the Feedback Governance section change how QA evaluates post-market data and when CAPAs are initiated. - QM.SLQ037: The revised Quality Objectives SOP introduces quarterly trending requirements and escalation criteria that affect the management team and QA. Interactive training at a management briefing is recommended. Effectiveness check: does management understand the new review cadence and escalation triggers?
- QM.SLQ040 and FM1-QM.SLQ040: The universal NCMR documentation requirement and the use-as-is risk assessment requirement change daily practice for Operations and QA. Interactive training is recommended for all personnel who initiate or approve NCMRs. Effectiveness check: can trainees correctly complete the risk assessment section of FM1-QM.SLQ040 for a use-as-is scenario?
- QM.SLQ047: The new validation applicability determination requirement changes how Operations and QA document decisions not to validate. Interactive training for relevant engineers and QA is recommended. Effectiveness check: can trainees correctly apply the objective criteria to determine whether a given process requires validation?
- QM.SLQ050 and FM4-QM.SLQ050: The out-of-tolerance handling process is substantively revised. Operations and QA personnel who use calibrated equipment must understand the new requirement to initiate FM4 for all out-of-tolerance events and to identify affected product lots. Interactive training is recommended. Effectiveness check: can trainees correctly complete the affected product traceability section of FM4-QM.SLQ050?
- QM.SLQ011: The documented justification requirement for non-application of statistical methods is new content but narrow in scope. Read-and-acknowledge with an effectiveness check (confirming trainees can cite where in the protocol to document the justification) is sufficient.
- QM.SLQ026: The regulatory traceability linkage section is new content relevant to the document administrator and engineering. Read-and-acknowledge with effectiveness check is appropriate.
- EDMS-only SOPs (QM.SLQ029, QM.SLQ038, QM.SLQ039, QM.SLQ043, QM.SLQ045, QM.SLQ046, QM.SLQ049, QM.SLQ051): Changes are limited to substituting Silq eQMS filing paths for FileHold references. Personnel already use Silq eQMS per prior DCO training. Read-and-acknowledge only; no effectiveness check required.

Training methodology and any comprehension assessment shall follow QM.SLQ003 Employee Training SOP. Schedule training to complete before the proposed effective dates in Part 5.

## Part 11 — After Approval: Publication in Silq eQMS

After all approvals are complete, the document administrator publishes each line item. These steps follow QM.SLQ001 Rev B and QM.SLQ014 Rev C.

1. For each row on the executed DCO, read the Document / Part Number, the new revision, and the final approved Proposed effective date.
2. In Silq eQMS Document Control, open that document number and create or select the draft revision matching the DCO.
3. On Release revision, set the Effective date to the same calendar date recorded for that row on the executed DCO. Do not apply any default offset from the approval date.
4. Confirm the Group A items (QM.SLQ027 and QM.SLQ048) carry the same effective date.
5. Confirm the Group B items (QM.SLQ050 and FM4-QM.SLQ050) carry the same effective date.
6. Confirm the Group C items (QM.SLQ040 and FM1-QM.SLQ040) carry the same effective date.
7. Notify the Training Coordinator to initiate the training assignments recorded in Part 7 so that training completes before the effective dates.
8. File the executed DCO094 PDF in the Completed DCO location named in QM.SLQ001 so that the DCO row dates can be compared to the Silq eQMS revision effective dates.
9. Confirm that the QM.SLQ027 Rev F Silq eQMS entry references or links to the Medical Device File Index in Silq eQMS Admin Docs, Device Master Records, for each device type.
