## Part 3 - Description and Reason for Change

One or two sentences per row. Detailed content is in the editing guide.

QM.SLQ052 Design Control SOP (New, Rev A)
Description: New consolidated Design Control SOP replacing QM.SLQ004 through QM.SLQ010. Defines Pathway A (three-gate full development), Pathway B (design change), and Pathway C (supplier or CMO-initiated change with mandatory trigger and hold decision). Includes conflict resolution authority, V&V planning and validation equivalence requirements, formal design output control, risk management integration, final design review requirement at transfer, DHF lifecycle in SILQ eQMS, and Medical Device File framework per ISO 13485 clause 4.2.3.
Reason: IA-2025 mNC 1 (all nine sub-issues); CAPA 004-2025 Action 5 (closes on DCO095 effective date); FDA 483 Observation 2 and CAPA 2025-003 design-control corrective; 2026 Quality Plan; DC.SLQ002 Phase 1B and Phase 2 FileHold-reference elimination.

FM1-QM.SLQ052 Design Project Scope and Plan (New, Rev A)
Description: New scalable form merging FM1-QM.SLQ004 and TMP1-QM.SLQ005. Includes mandatory V&V planning determination field with QA approval (closes mNC 1 sub-issue 7) and mandatory plan-revision change-control log (closes mNC 1 sub-issue 3).
Reason: IA-2025 mNC 1 sub-issues 2, 3, and 7; operationalizes QM.SLQ052 Rev A.

FM2-QM.SLQ052 Design Change Assessment (New, Rev A)
Description: New mandatory change assessment record for Pathways B and C. Captures change source, hold decision (Pathway C), design impact, risk determination, V&V determination, regulatory assessment (Letter-to-File or 510(k) retained in DHF), and routing to the DCO process.
Reason: IA-2025 mNC 1 sub-issues 1, 6, 7, 8, 9; FDA 483 Observation 2 and CAPA 2025-003 supplier change trigger; IA-2025 mNC 3.

FM3-QM.SLQ052 Design Review Record (New, Rev A)
Description: New scalable design review form with gate selector (Gate 1, 2, 3, and Fast-Track) replacing FM1-QM.SLQ008. Includes required attendee list, gate-specific deliverable checklist, action items, conclusion, and DHF closure block. Adapted from consultant review forms FORM 7-05 through 7-09 and FORM 7-23.
Reason: IA-2025 mNC 1 sub-issues 2, 5, and 9; operationalizes QM.SLQ052 Rev A Section 12.

FM4-QM.SLQ052 Design V&V Deviation (New, Rev A)
Description: Replaces FM1-QM.SLQ009. Same functional content plus explicit prohibition on using the form to change acceptance criteria.
Reason: Consolidation into QM.SLQ052 family; operationalizes QM.SLQ052 Rev A Section 13.6.

TMP1-QM.SLQ052 Design Input, Output, and Traceability Matrix (New, Rev A)
Description: New traceability matrix (6 columns) replacing TMP1-QM.SLQ006, TMP2-QM.SLQ006, and TMP2-QM.SLQ007. Consolidates user needs, design inputs, design output references, V&V protocol references, status, and notes into one controlled document.
Reason: IA-2025 OFI 2 (traceability of risk to design controls and V&V); IA-2025 mNC 1; operationalizes QM.SLQ052 Rev A Section 17.

TMP2-QM.SLQ052 V&V Plan, Protocol, and Report (New, Rev A)
Description: Replaces TMP1, TMP2, and TMP3-QM.SLQ009 in a single three-part document (Plan, Protocol, Final Report). Includes lean test group summary table, validation equivalence documentation, statistical rationale, results table, and separate approval actions for protocol and report.
Reason: IA-2025 mNC 1 sub-issues 7 and 8; operationalizes QM.SLQ052 Rev A Section 13.

TMP3-QM.SLQ052 Design Transfer Checklist (New, Rev A)
Description: Replaces TMP1-QM.SLQ010 (clinical) and TMP2-QM.SLQ010 (commercial) in a single scalable checklist. Includes explicit statement that the checklist does not substitute for Gate 3 or Fast-Track design review.
Reason: IA-2025 mNC 1 sub-issue 9; operationalizes QM.SLQ052 Rev A Section 15.

QM.SLQ027 Quality Manual (E to F)
Description: Replaced seven-SOP design control listing with QM.SLQ052. Updated Appendix 1 ISO 13485 clause 7.3 row to reference QM.SLQ052 and new forms. Updated Clauses 5.2, 7.1, and 7.2 rows. Updated Customer Focus and Design and Development Process sections.
Reason: Cross-reference alignment with DCO095; IA-2025 mNC 1.
