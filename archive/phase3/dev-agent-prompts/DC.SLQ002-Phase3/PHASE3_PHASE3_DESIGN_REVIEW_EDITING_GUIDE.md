Title: Known Limitations and Risks
Formatting note: Bullet slide. Four bullets. Mirror the tone and structure of the Phase 0 slide 4 (frank, concise, risk-accepting where appropriate). No tables needed.

Paste block:
---PASTE START---
Known Limitations and Risks

Silq eQMS is intentionally not a full Part 11 system: there are no electronic signatures and no in-app DCO approval workflow. Formal approvals are executed outside Silq eQMS, as stated in SW.SLQ008 and consistent with QM.SLQ001 (Document Control SOP). This is a known and accepted design characteristic.

Record integrity risks (loss, corruption, unauthorized access, misuse of obsolete records) are addressed by role-based access control, nightly backups by DigitalOcean, cryptographic hashing, a no-delete design, and a robust append-only audit trail. Obsolete documents display a warning to users.

No open, known issues were identified that affect the security, functionality, or intended use of Silq eQMS as SILQ's Electronic Document Management System.

Staff onboarding is gated on Phase 4 completion: no team members have been provided access. Users will be onboarded only after completing their employee training on the revised QM procedures and the Silq eQMS system, per the DC.SLQ002 project plan.
---PASTE END---
