

Design Project Plan


SilqQMS Electronic Document Management System Transition Plan


Purpose
The purpose of this design control project plan is to define the activities, reviews, deliverables, and resources necessary to transition SILQ's quality document operations from the FileHold document management system to the SilqQMS electronic document management system. This plan ensures the transition is conducted in compliance with QM.SLQ005 (Design Project Planning SOP), QM.SLQ001 (Document Control SOP), ISO 13485:2016, Medical Devices — Quality Management Systems, and 21 CFR Part 820 Quality Management System Regulation
Scope
This document is the QMS platform transition plan for moving SILQ's quality document operations from the FileHold document management system to the SilqQMS electronic document management system. Because many current QM procedures explicitly describe FileHold specific workflows, every FileHold reference must be evaluated and updated.
SilqQMS software validation is being conducted per QM.SLQ032 (Software Validation SOP) and is documented in the SW.SLQ007–012 validation deliverable series. The activities described in this transition plan are to be initiated upon completion of the SilqQMS validation

General Definitions
SilqQMS: A custom-developed, web-based electronic document management system (EDMS) used by SILQ to store, organize, retrieve, and lifecycle-manage controlled QMS documents and records.
FileHold: Software-based document management system previously used to electronically store controlled documents.
Document Change Order (DCO): The form used to add new or change existing documents in the document control system, per QM.SLQ001.
Design History File (DHF): A compilation of records that describes the design history of a finished device or system.
Product Overview
SILQ's existing quality procedures are well-structured, operationally detailed, and reflect years of established practice. However, many procedures describe FileHold-specific workflows and UI mechanics. As SILQ transitions its electronic document management to SilqQMS, these procedures must be updated to reflect the new system's operations.
SilqQMS is a custom-developed, web-based EDMS that acts in support of SILQ's paper-based QMS. Documents are authored and approved outside of SilqQMS (per QM.SLQ001 Document Control SOP) and uploaded to SilqQMS for controlled storage, revision tracking, and retrieval. The SilqQMS validation is being conducted per QM.SLQ032 and is documented in the SW.SLQ007–012 validation deliverable series. The system provides: Controlled document storage with formal revision tracking (Draft → Released → Obsolete lifecycle), Eleven browsable document libraries organized by QMS subsystem, Append-only audit trail for compliance evidence, Role-based access control for document security
The transition described in this plan consists of updating QMS procedure text to replace FileHold-specific instructions with SilqQMS-equivalent operations. It does not change the substance of SILQ's quality procedures — only how they describe the system used to manage documents.
Product Quality
Project will be implemented to provide deliverables in compliance with the regulations set forth in ISO 13485:2016 (Medical Devices — Quality Management Systems — Requirements for Regulatory Purposes) and 21 CFR Part 820, Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR, as revised — incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by reference).
Key Milestones
This project consists of five sequential phases following the completion of SilqQMS software validation (SW.SLQ007–012):
Phase 1A QM Document Major Revisions: Complete rewrite of the two procedures with deep FileHold dependency (QM.SLQ014, QM.SLQ001). These are the foundational documents that define system workflows and document control operations. Completing these first establishes shared transition language for subsequent phases.
Phase 1B QM Document Targeted Revisions: Line-by-line review and revision of six procedures with multiple embedded FileHold references in process steps (QM.SLQ003, QM.SLQ017, QM.SLQ020, QM.SLQ036, QM.SLQ015, QM.SLQ004). Each parent SOP revision includes review of associated forms and templates.
Phase 2 QM Document Minor Revisions: Batch review and update of 25 procedures with 1–2 FileHold references (typically glossary definitions and records-filing sentences).
Phase 3 Data Migration: Transfer of existing files, records, and document archives from FileHold into SilqQMS.
Phase 4 Employee Training: Training of all personnel on revised quality system procedures and the SilqQMS system
