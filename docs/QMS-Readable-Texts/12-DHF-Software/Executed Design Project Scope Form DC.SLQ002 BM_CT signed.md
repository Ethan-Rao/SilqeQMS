SILQ's current Electronic Document Management System, FileHold is being replaced
by SilqQMS, a custom-developed web application. The transition is driven by the
need for a modernized EDMS tailored to SILQ's document control workflows and
ensure the Silq EDMS is compliant with updates to regulatory requirements since
the previous EDMS validation performed in 2022.
Reason for Project Initiation:
The project will entail execution of compliance activities to satisfy requirements for
validated QMS software prior to use per ISO 13485:2016 clause 4.1.6, 21 CFR Part
820 Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR), and QM.SLQ032. After
validation and verification requirements are met, FileHold specific Silq procedures
will be updated to reflect SilqQMS specific or platform independent procedures.
The only external dependency is the hosting provider. There is no external software
