# Concise editing guide: Silq design review slide deck

Purpose: Keep the presentation short. Attendees already have the documents. The deck orients the room, shows where each record lives, and lists decisions or questions. It does not replace reading the PDFs or Word files.

Style reference: Your earlier deck Silq Design Review Meeting 5.8.pptx (same spirit: simple titles, few bullets, visual anchors).

Suggested length: 8 to 12 slides for a one-hour meeting. Fewer is fine if the scope is narrow.

Visual rule: Use screenshots or exported images of the first page or table of contents of each controlled document. Do not paste full procedures into slides. Legible title block plus document ID and revision is enough.

Tone: Plain English. One idea per bullet. No regulatory jargon unless the audience expects it.

---

## Recommended slide order

Slide 1 — Title  

Paste-style content:  

Meeting title (example): Silq Design Review  

Subtitle line: Project name (example: DC.SLQ002 — SilqQMS EDMS Transition)  

Footer line: Date, time zone, moderator name  

Optional small line: In person or video link  

Slide 2 — Why we are here  

Three bullets maximum.  

Example bullets (edit for your meeting):  

We are meeting to confirm the design package for [project] before sign-off.  

Everyone received the documents in advance; this session is for questions and alignment.  

Outcome today: list approved actions, open questions, or both.  

Slide 3 — Agenda  

Four to six bullets only. Match the agenda on your FM1-QM.SLQ008 minutes form if you use one.  

Example pattern:  

Introductions and quorum  

Walk-through of deliverables (by document)  

Risk or known-issues discussion if applicable  

Decisions, action items, next steps  

Slide 4 — Documents reviewed (overview)  

One compact table or a row of small thumbnails. Columns: Document ID, Short title, Revision.  

Example rows you might list for a SilqQMS validation close-out (replace with your actual list):  

SW.SLQ007 A — Validation Plan  

SW.SLQ008 A — Product Requirements Specification  

SW.SLQ009 A — Verification Test Plan  

SW.SLQ010 A — Verification Test Procedure executed  

SW.SLQ011 A — Validation Report draft or final  

SW.SLQ012 A — Requirements Traceability Matrix  

Optional supporting records: technical review minutes for known issues, designation file for Git commit  

Slide 5 through N — One slide per major theme (optional)  

Use this pattern only when a theme needs explanation beyond a screenshot.  

Title: Short theme name (example: Verification results).  

Body: Two or four bullets in plain English.  

Optional: one screenshot (test summary page, traceability excerpt, or report conclusion paragraph).  

Do not duplicate more than one half-page of any Word document on a single slide.  

Example theme titles for software validation:  

What we tested (high-level: Document Control, Admin Docs, security, audit)  

How we know it matches requirements (point to SW.SLQ012, not full matrix on screen)  

Known issues review (point to minutes attachment; one sentence conclusion)  

Slide — Risks, gaps, or exceptions (only if needed)  

If nothing is open: one slide with a single sentence: No blocking issues identified for this package.  

If something is open: table with Issue, Owner, Due date. Keep to three rows or fewer on screen; attach detail in the packet.  

Slide — Decisions and action items  

Bullets: What was approved, what needs a follow-up owner, what goes to DCO or filing.  

Mirror what will go into meeting minutes.  

Final slide — Close  

Thank you and contact line for questions after the meeting.  

Optional: reference where the signed minutes will be filed.  

---

## Screenshot tips

Export PDF first page or Word print preview at readable zoom. Crop whitespace.  

Put the document number and revision in the slide title so the image is not the only label.  

If you show Git commit information, use the same SHA as your VALIDATED_CONFIGURATION_ITEM_DESIGNATION.md file so slides match the reports.  

---

## Speaker notes

One or two sentences per slide in the notes pane pointing to file path or SharePoint link. Do not read slides word for word if the room has pre-read.  

---

## After the meeting

Save the deck next to the meeting minutes as a non-controlled reference unless your procedure requires controlling presentations.  

Filename pattern: Silq Design Review Meeting YYYY-MM-DD.pptx
